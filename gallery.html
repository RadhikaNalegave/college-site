MKSSS’s Cummins College of Engineering for Women, Pune

Cummins College is the first all women engineering college in India established in the year 1991 with support of Cummins Diesel (India) Foundation. Parent institution of Cummins College is Maharshi Karve Stree Shikshan Samstha (MKSSS). ‘Bharat Ratna’ Recipient, Maharshi Karve, established MKSSS in 1896 with the mission of women empowerment through education.
